# 🚀 StudyReviewBlast

**An open-source spaced repetition and dynamic quiz platform built to maximize study retention.**

---

## 📖 About the Project

**StudyReviewBlast** is built around spaced repetition mechanics inspired by modern cognitive science and tools like Anki. Its core mission is to transform passive reviewing into high-yield, active retention.

The platform's main differentiator is the ability to construct **highly customizable quizzes** organized in focused study blocks (sets of 5 or 10 questions). By continuously monitoring your performance history, StudyReviewBlast dynamically adapts your learning pathway:

- **Weak points and frequent errors are automatically funneled to the top** of your study queue for immediate reinforcement.
- **Concepts and vocabulary you have already mastered are spaced out**, preserving your cognitive bandwidth for what truly needs attention.
- **Continuous, adaptive repetition** guarantees that learning never hits a dead end, allowing you to train until application becomes second nature.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 🃏 Custom Quiz Creation and Editing
- **Tailored Quizzes:** Create, adjust, and edit your own questions to challenge the practical application of verbs, sentences, and contextual nuances.
- **Modular Block Sizing:** Structure review sessions into rapid 5-question sprints or deep-dive 10-question blocks to match your daily schedule and focus level.
- **Direct Practical Application:** Test real-world usage rather than isolated definitions, ensuring knowledge transfers effectively to active communication.

### 🎯 Error Funneling Algorithm
- **Automated Weak-Item Prioritization:** The algorithm computes difficulty, error frequency, and time since last exposure to push cards with higher error rates straight to the front of the queue.
- **Dynamic Queue Reordering:** As you conquer challenging items, their priority decreases, gracefully moving them to longer review intervals while surfacing unresolved items.
- **Targeted Learning Paths:** Filter sessions by specific items, difficulty tiers, or mastery levels (e.g., focus strictly on weak items or consolidate intermediate items).

### 🧠 Active Recall Validation & Self-Assessment
- **Brain-First Retrieval:** Force cognitive recall by confronting the challenge before revealing the answer.
- **Immediate Self-Grading:** Evaluate each response directly using three clear feedback tiers:
  - 🔴 **Hard / Error:** Needs immediate repetition and error analysis.
  - 🟡 **Partial:** Recognized but requires consolidation.
  - 🟢 **Easy / Correct:** Mastered and ready for extended spacing intervals.
- **Audit & Transparency:** Clear visibility into why an item was chosen for review and how your rating influences its next review schedule.

### 💬 Context-Driven Learning & Sentence Recall
- **Real-World Contexts:** Group practice around authentic situations, common collocations, and idiomatic patterns.
- **Sentence-Level Testing:** Reinforce verbs through complete sentence structures, grammatical tenses, and auditory feedback to internalize natural rhythm and pronunciation.
- **Granular Editing:** Edit sentences, definitions, and usage notes directly in place as your understanding deepens.

---

## 🎯 Pedagogical Philosophy

1. **Active Retrieval over Passive Recognition:** True retention happens when the brain works to pull information out, not when it simply recognizes it on a page.
2. **Spaced Distribution:** Reviews occur at strategically increasing intervals to solidify memories right at the threshold of forgetting.
3. **Continuous Mastery:** Practice is not capped by arbitrary daily limits. You retain the freedom to practice continuously, hone specific items, or run custom quizzes whenever inspiration or need arises.
